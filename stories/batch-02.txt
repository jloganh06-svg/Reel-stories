=== 12 the-toast ===

He was going to marry me and keep her.

I wasn't going to cancel. Two hundred guests, fourteen thousand dollars, and my grandmother already on a plane from Ohio.

=== 13 uncle-ray ===
I took a DNA test for fun and found out my uncle is my real father. Then I found out my family has known for 26 years.

I'm 26, and for Christmas my sister got us both one of those DNA kits. My dad's family is huge on being Irish. There's a flag in the garage and a framed family tree in the hallway. I figured it would be fun.

My results came back on a Tuesday. The ancestry part looked normal. Then I clicked the relatives tab.

The first match was my sister, Erin. But it didn't say sister. It said half sister.

I figured that was a glitch. Then I saw the second match.

It was my uncle Ray. My dad's younger brother. The fun uncle. The one who taught me to fish and showed up to every one of my softball games.

Next to his name, it didn't say uncle. It said parent, child.

I read it about twenty times. I logged out and logged back in. I spent an hour googling whether a test could mix up an uncle and a father. At that amount of shared DNA, it can't.

Ray had taken the same test two years ago. He was the one who made the framed family tree. The moment I joined, my name would have popped up on his account as his daughter.

He might have known before I did. Or, I started to realize, he'd known a lot longer than that.

That Sunday, I went to family dinner like normal. My dad carved the roast. Ray sat across from me and asked how work was going.

Then my mom looked at Ray and said, "Did you ever finish that family tree?"

And Ray looked straight at me.
